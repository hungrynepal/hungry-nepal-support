## 1. Introduction

Hungry Hetauda Private Limited ("Hungry Hetauda", "Company", "we", "us", or "our") respects your right to control your personal information.

This Account & Data Deletion Policy explains how users can request deletion of their Hungry Hetauda account and associated personal information.

This policy applies to users of the Hungry Hetauda mobile application, website, and related digital services.

---

## 2. How to Delete Your Account

Users can request account deletion directly from the Hungry Hetauda application where account creation is available.

### In-App Account Deletion

The typical account deletion path is:

**Profile → Settings → Account → Delete Account**

The exact location of the account deletion option may change as the application is updated.

Users may be required to confirm their identity or enter their password, OTP, or another authentication method before deletion is completed.

---

## 3. Deletion Request by Email

If the in-app deletion option is unavailable or the user cannot access their account, the user may request account deletion by contacting:

**Email:** [contact@hungryheatauda.com](mailto:contact@hungryheatauda.com)

Users should use the email subject:

**Account Deletion Request**

The request should include sufficient information for us to identify the relevant account.

For security purposes, we may request reasonable verification before deleting an account.

---

## 4. What Happens After an Account Deletion Request

After receiving a valid deletion request, Hungry Hetauda may:

1. Verify the identity of the account owner;
2. Review any active orders or operational matters;
3. Disable the account;
4. Delete personal information that is eligible for deletion;
5. Anonymize information where deletion is not technically or legally appropriate;
6. Retain information that is required by law or reasonably necessary for legitimate legal or security purposes;
7. Remove the account from active systems;
8. Allow remaining backup copies to be removed through the normal backup-retention lifecycle.

---

## 5. Information That May Be Deleted

Subject to applicable law and technical requirements, account deletion may result in deletion or anonymization of information such as:

* Full name;
* Email address;
* Mobile phone number;
* Login credentials;
* Profile information;
* Saved delivery addresses;
* Saved preferences;
* Favorites;
* Account settings;
* Marketing preferences;
* Other non-essential account information.

---

## 6. Order and Transaction Information

Deleting an account does not necessarily mean that every record associated with the account will be immediately deleted.

Certain information may need to be retained for purposes including:

* Tax compliance;
* Accounting;
* Payment reconciliation;
* Financial records;
* Fraud prevention;
* Security;
* Dispute resolution;
* Legal claims;
* Regulatory requirements.

Where such information is retained, Hungry Hetauda will limit its use to the purposes for which retention is necessary.

---

## 7. Backup Information

Deleted information may temporarily remain in secure backup systems.

Backups are maintained for purposes including:

* Disaster recovery;
* Business continuity;
* Security;
* System restoration.

Backup copies are not intended for ordinary operational use.

Such information will be removed or overwritten according to the applicable backup-retention and rotation process.

---

## 8. Active Orders

If an account has an active food order, delivery, refund, payment, complaint, or other ongoing transaction, certain information may need to be retained until the relevant process is completed.

This does not prevent us from deleting information that is no longer required for the relevant purpose.

---

## 9. Legally Required Information

Some information may be retained where required or permitted by applicable law.

Examples may include information necessary for:

* Tax records;
* Accounting records;
* Financial reconciliation;
* Fraud prevention;
* Security investigations;
* Legal proceedings;
* Regulatory compliance;
* Enforcement of legal rights.

Retained information will not be used for unrelated purposes merely because it remains in our records.

---

## 10. Data That May Be Retained for Security

Hungry Hetauda may retain limited technical or transactional information where reasonably necessary to:

* Prevent fraud;
* Investigate suspicious activity;
* Prevent repeated abuse;
* Protect customer accounts;
* Protect the platform;
* Investigate security incidents;
* Comply with legal obligations.

Such information will be retained only for an appropriate period based on the relevant purpose.

---

## 11. Reviews and User-Generated Content

If a user has submitted reviews, ratings, photographs, complaints, or other user-generated content, deletion of the account may not automatically remove every piece of content where the content:

* Has been anonymized;
* Is necessary for legal or security purposes;
* Has already been incorporated into records that must be retained;
* Is otherwise permitted to be retained under applicable law.

Where technically and legally appropriate, personal information associated with deleted accounts will be removed or anonymized.

---

## 12. Deletion of Personal Information Without Account Deletion

Where applicable, users may request deletion of specific categories of personal information without deleting their entire account.

Such requests may be sent to:

**[contact@hungryheatauda.com](mailto:contact@hungryheatauda.com)**

We will assess the request in accordance with applicable law and our Privacy Policy.

---

## 13. Verification of Deletion Requests

To protect customer accounts, Hungry Hetauda may require reasonable verification before processing an account or data deletion request.

Verification may include information such as:

* Registered email address;
* Registered mobile number;
* Account-related information;
* Authentication through the application;
* Other reasonable verification methods.

We will not intentionally disclose or delete another person's account based solely on an unverified request.

---

## 14. Processing Time

We aim to process valid account deletion requests within a reasonable period.

The actual processing time may depend on:

* Identity verification;
* Account status;
* Active orders;
* Legal retention requirements;
* Technical requirements;
* Backup systems;
* Complexity of the request.

Where applicable law establishes a specific timeframe, that legal requirement will apply.

---

## 15. Consequences of Account Deletion

After an account is deleted:

* You may no longer be able to log in;
* Your saved addresses may no longer be available;
* Your favorites and preferences may be removed;
* Your account order history may no longer be accessible through the application;
* Personalized features may no longer be available;
* You may need to create a new account to use account-based features in the future.

Deleting an account does not necessarily cancel an already completed transaction or remove legally required transaction records.

---

## 16. Recreating an Account

After account deletion, a user may create a new Hungry Hetauda account in the future if account registration remains available.

A newly created account may be treated as a separate account and may not contain information from the previously deleted account.

---

## 17. Account Deletion Is Not the Same as App Uninstallation

Uninstalling the Hungry Hetauda application from a device does not necessarily delete the user's account or information stored on Hungry Hetauda's servers.

Users who want their account and eligible personal information deleted should use the in-app account deletion feature or contact us at:

**[contact@hungryheatauda.com](mailto:contact@hungryheatauda.com)**

---

## 18. Marketing Communications

Account deletion will stop account-based promotional communications where applicable.

However, communications relating to legally required records, security matters, or transactions may be retained or sent where necessary.

Users may also manage marketing preferences separately without deleting their account.

---

## 19. Google Play Account Deletion

Where the Hungry Hetauda application allows users to create accounts, Hungry Hetauda provides an account deletion mechanism consistent with applicable Google Play requirements.

The account deletion mechanism is intended to allow users to request deletion of their account and associated eligible data.

The deletion process may retain information that is legally required or reasonably necessary for security, fraud prevention, legal claims, financial records, or other permitted purposes.
